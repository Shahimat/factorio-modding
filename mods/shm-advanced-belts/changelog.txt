Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-10-25
  Features:
    - Four belt tiers beyond turbo: superior, ultra, hyper and absolute, 90, 105, 135 and 150 items/s, with undergrounds and splitters.
    - AAI Loaders for each tier when AAI Loaders is installed.
    - Startup settings for each tier: enabled, speed, underground length (15, 19, 23 and 27 by default).
    - Optional unlocking of all AAI loaders by the technologies of their belts.
    - AAI loader price divisor for the expensive mode of AAI Loaders.
    - Belt stacking for all loaders and a per-loader full stack mode: SHIFT + M or a switch next to the loader window.
    - API ShmAdvancedBelts for other mods.
  Locale:
    - English, Russian, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Polish, Ukrainian, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean.
  Compatibility:
    - Factorio 2.1, Space Age, AAI Loaders.
