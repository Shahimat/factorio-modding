Version: 1.0.1
Date: 2026-10-27
  Features:
    - Inserter ghosts can be switched between "Full hand" and "Instant": with the switch next to the ghost window or SHIFT + M, from any distance.
  Bugfixes:
    - The full hand switch is shown next to the inserter ghost window and stays visible when the ghost is built while its window is open.
    - An inserter built by hand over a ghost takes the ghost's mode instead of "Full hand".
  Locale:
    - Removed the unused "Cannot switch a ghost" message.

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-10-25
  Features:
    - Superior inserters and superior long inserters wait for a full hand before dropping.
    - Per-inserter switch between "Full hand" and "Instant": SHIFT + M or a switch next to the inserter window.
    - The mode is kept in blueprints and copied with entity settings.
  Locale:
    - English, Russian, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Polish, Ukrainian, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean.
  Compatibility:
    - Factorio 2.1, Krastorio 2, K2 Spaced Out, Change Inserter Drop Lane.
